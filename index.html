Journey guide
  1. Lie down somewhere comfortable and cover your eyes.
  2. Set a simple intention or question for the journey.
  3. Picture an opening into the earth that you know or imagine — a cave, a hollow tree, a crater or a lake — and go through it.
  4. Let the drum carry you. Notice what you see, hear and feel without steering it.
  5. When the fast rattle starts, retrace your path back through the opening and return.

Set a session timer to hear the rattle callback in the final 45 seconds.
